Quran Duo

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Quran Duo does not collect, store, or share any personal information. This page explains what that means in practice.

No data collection

The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. It never connects to the internet: the Quran text, translation, and fonts are all built into the app.

What stays on your device

To remember your reading, the app saves the following on your device only:

  • your reading position and display settings (such as text size and layout)
  • bookmarks and the notes you write on them
  • a short list of places you've recently read
  • your khatm plan and which pages you've marked as read

This information is never sent to us or anyone else. If your device is backed up to iCloud or a computer, it's included in that backup in the same way as other app data, under Apple's terms. Deleting the app removes it.

Children

Because the app collects no information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, for example if a future version adds optional syncing, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a new date, and the change will be described in the app's release notes.
