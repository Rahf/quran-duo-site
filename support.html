Common questions

How do I see two pages side by side?

Unfold your iPhone Duo (or turn an iPad to landscape), then tap the layout button at the top right and choose Two pages. The same menu has Page and translation, Memorise and Scroll.

How do I bookmark a verse or add a note?

In the scrolling reader, long-press a verse. On a mushaf page, tap a verse. Choose a colour, and add a note if you like. Your bookmarks are in the Saved tab of the index.

How does memorising work?

Choose Memorise from the layout button. Every verse on the page is covered. Recite, then tap a verse to check it, or use Reveal next verse. Turn on First-word hints to see the beginning of each verse.

How are khatm pages counted?

A page counts as read when you've spent a little time on it and then read on to the next page. You can also tick pages off yourself in the khatm plan, or mark the whole day's portion as read.

Will I lose my bookmarks if I change phones?

Your bookmarks and progress are stored on your device and are included in your device backups. Restoring a backup to a new phone brings them with it.
